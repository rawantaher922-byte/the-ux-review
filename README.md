# the-ux-review
A bold, editorial-style landing page for The UX Review, a blog focused on technology, design, and digital culture. The page introduces the blog, highlights recent articles, showcases its authors, and invites visitors to join its community.
Features
- Navigation Bar: Links to the main areas of the blog.
- Hero Section: Bold headline, introduction, and featured image.
- Latest Articles: Article cards with categories, author names, dates, reading times, and likes.
- Trending Articles: A sidebar highlighting popular content.
- Categories: A quick overview of the blog's content categories.
- Meet the Authors: Author profile cards with short biographies and social links.
- Community Section: Community introduction, benefits, and testimonials.
- Newsletter Area: Email subscription interface in the footer.
Technologies Used
- HTML5 – Page structure and content.
- CSS3 – Styling and layout.
- Google Fonts (Exo) – Typography.
- Image Assets – Article thumbnails, author photos, and icons.

Getting Started
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the project folder in your code editor.
3. Open index.html in your browser.
Keep the css and images folders in their expected locations so the page can load its styles and image assets correctly.
